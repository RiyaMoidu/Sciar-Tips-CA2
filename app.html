Your voice matters

Ask about your tips, hours or the distribution rule. Your role should not prevent you from raising a concern.

Simulation only. Concerns are saved in this browser, not sent to Sciar or Halloran’s. Anyone with access to this browser may see them. Do not enter names, contact details or sensitive information. Use sample text only.

Design rationale: no automatic sharing with management, to respect the employee's request for confidentiality and avoid exposing them to unwanted escalation. A real service would need secure access and an agreed escalation process.
